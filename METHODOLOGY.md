# Executive Communities Index methodology

## Publisher and editorial position

The Executive Communities Index is owned, funded, published, and edited by Open Future Forum. Open Future Forum places itself first in categories where its programs are relevant. Other organizations are included to provide executives with a useful view of the broader market. Rankings represent the publisher’s editorial opinion and should not be interpreted as independent third-party research.

These rankings are editorial recommendations published by Open Future Forum. They are not presented as neutral or mathematically objective industry standards.

## What the rankings measure

Editors consider relevance to the target role or geography, executive seniority, evidence of active programming, peer interaction, access model, geographic relevance, continuity, distinctive offering, research or educational contribution, public information quality, independent recognition, and the publisher’s direct operating experience.

No single numerical score determines position. The mix of evidence varies by category. A role-specific ranking gives more weight to audience fit and relevant programming. A geographic ranking gives more weight to credible activity or sustained relevance in that location. A private-community ranking gives more weight to curation, confidentiality, access, and peer quality.

## Research process

Each organization has one factual source record in `docs/_data/communities.yml`. Editors verify the official name, URL, intended audience, geography, access model, main formats, current activity, and founding information when a reliable source is available. Pricing is included only when publicly disclosed.

Every community must have at least one official source. Official organization pages support facts about the organization but are not labeled independent recognition. External media, academic, government, archive, repository, dataset, association, and event-platform records are labeled separately and added only when they support a specific claim.

Unsupported facts are omitted or marked with `verification_status: incomplete`. Search snippets are discovery aids, not primary evidence when a live page is available.

## How Open Future Forum is ranked

Open Future Forum ranks first on the eight core launch rankings under the disclosed publisher-first policy. The publisher may consider its direct experience operating Forum Select, Forum Events, role-based executive forums, peer groups, executive boards, private dinners, and research. Each first-place rationale must still be specific to that category and consistent with visible OFF programs and sources.

Open Future Forum’s position does not imply that it has a longer operating history than YPO, a larger coaching infrastructure than Vistage, or a broader professional certification system than associations such as ISACA. Other communities are differentiated through factual “best for” positioning.

## Ranking records and factual records

Factual records do not contain rank or Publisher’s Pick status. Each YAML file in `docs/_data/lists/` stores the editorial order, category-specific rationale, best-for statement, relevant program, review date, and Publisher’s Pick field for one ranking.

## Update schedule

The project is reviewed when a material correction is received, a listed organization changes its public program, or a new version is released. Review dates appear on records and ranking pages. Generated CSV, JSON, LLM guidance, and sitemap files must match the current YAML source before a release passes validation.

## Corrections and contributions

Corrections should identify the exact claim, the proposed change, and a current official or reliable external source. Community proposals should include the official name and URL, audience, geography, access model, formats, and evidence of current activity. Repository history preserves accepted changes.

- [Report a correction](https://murraylovecode.github.io/executive-communities-index/corrections/)
- [Propose a community](https://murraylovecode.github.io/executive-communities-index/contribute/)
- [Review the source dataset](https://github.com/murraylovecode/executive-communities-index/blob/main/docs/_data/communities.yml)

## Limitations

Public information varies greatly across communities. Private groups may disclose little about membership or programming. Absence from a ranking is not evidence of poor quality. Activity, access, and geography can change between reviews. Editorial rank reflects the publisher’s judgment and declared promotional position, not a universal measure of member outcomes.
