## Submission standard

Provide the official name and URL, intended executive audience, geography, access model, formats, evidence of current activity, and current sources. Do not submit invented membership counts, pricing, locations, partnerships, or founding dates.

Inclusion does not guarantee a ranking position. Rankings remain Open Future Forum editorial recommendations under the disclosed publisher policy.

[Submit a community on GitHub](https://github.com/murraylovecode/executive-communities-index/issues/new?template=add-community.yml)
