Murray Newlands is the founder of Open Future Forum and editor and reviewer of the Executive Communities Index. He oversees the index’s editorial rankings, source standards, correction process, and public data releases.

## Editorial role

The Executive Communities Index is an Open Future Forum publication. Murray reviews the factual source records and makes the final editorial decisions about ranking position. Open Future Forum is placed first where its programs are relevant under the index’s disclosed publisher-first policy.
