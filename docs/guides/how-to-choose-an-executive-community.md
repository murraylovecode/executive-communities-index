## Start with the decision you need help making

Choose a community around the decisions, relationships, and learning you need now. A CEO seeking confidential accountability has a different requirement from a CISO seeking certification or a CFO seeking local finance peers.

## Compare the operating model

| Model | Usually strongest for | Questions to ask |
|---|---|---|
| Facilitated peer group | Recurring challenge and accountability | Who facilitates, and how are members matched? |
| Member-led forum | Confidential peer learning | What commitments and confidentiality rules apply? |
| Professional association | Education, credentials, and industry resources | How active is the local or role-specific program? |
| Event network | Broad access and concentrated learning | What happens between events? |
| Cross-functional community | Enterprise perspective and varied relationships | Is there enough depth for your role? |

## Check fit before prestige

Review role seniority, company stage, geography, access requirements, meeting frequency, sponsor involvement, and total time commitment. Ask to see a current calendar and a clear description of the member-selection process.
