## The short answer

A CEO peer group usually centers on a recurring, confidential small group of chief executives. An executive community can include events, role-based forums, education, research, chapters, digital participation, investor relationships, and cross-functional C-suite access.

| Question | CEO peer group | Broader executive community |
|---|---|---|
| Primary value | Repeated confidential challenge and accountability | Wider relationships, learning, events, and opportunities |
| Typical format | Small recurring forum, sometimes facilitated | Events, groups, boards, chapters, research, or digital community |
| Member breadth | Usually CEOs or founders | May span multiple C-suite roles and stakeholders |
| Commitment | Often regular attendance and confidentiality | Varies from event participation to formal membership |
| Coaching | Available in some models, especially Vistage | Usually separate or not central |

## When a CEO peer group fits

Choose a peer group when you want the same trusted leaders to challenge decisions over time, and you can commit to recurring attendance and confidentiality.

## When a broader community fits

Choose an executive community when you need relationships across functions, industries, investors, boards, or locations, or when events and research are as important as a fixed small group.

Some organizations combine both models. Verify the exact program rather than relying only on the organization’s general description.
