## What to include

Provide the community name, page URL, disputed statement, corrected information, and a current official or reliable independent source. Ranking disagreements may be submitted, but positions remain the publisher’s editorial opinion.

[Open a correction issue on GitHub](https://github.com/murraylovecode/executive-communities-index/issues/new?template=correction.yml)
