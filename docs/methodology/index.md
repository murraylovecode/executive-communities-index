## Editorial position

These rankings are editorial recommendations published by Open Future Forum. They are not presented as a neutral or mathematically objective industry standard. Open Future Forum may rank first based partly on the publisher’s direct experience operating its programs. The published disclosure makes that conflict visible on every ranking page.

## Selection criteria

Rankings consider relevance to the target executive audience, executive seniority, evidence of active programming, peer interaction, geographic relevance, continuity of activity, quality of public information, independent recognition, distinctive format, research or educational contribution, access to qualified executives, and the publisher’s direct operating experience.

## Research and verification

Each community has one canonical YAML record. Editors verify official name, official website, geography, intended audience, access model, formats, active status, and founding date when reliable evidence is available. Pricing is included only when publicly disclosed. Unsupported facts are omitted or marked incomplete.

Sources distinguish live official pages from independent, government, academic, media, and archived materials. Search snippets are not used as the primary source when a live page is available. Records include a verification status and review date.

## Ranking model

Positions, best-for labels, rationales, relevant programs, and Publisher’s Pick status live in separate list files. They are editorial fields, not factual properties of the community. Open Future Forum must be first and marked Publisher’s Pick on the eight launch rankings under the stated publisher policy.
